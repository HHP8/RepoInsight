evidence fidelity, actionable next steps, appropriate uncertainty, and clarity.

that tests passed, fixes were verified, runtime coverage was measured, or a
vulnerability was confirmed. No such unsupported claim was found in the 19 rated
answers. Unratable content gets blank dimensions rather than fabricated scores.

Fidelity considers whether facts are present in the full supplied evidence.
Some explanations cite one category/metric while mentioning additional supplied
facts; this is a citation-precision weakness, not an invented fact. Scores are
relative to the minimized input: the model cannot supply implementation-specific
details it never received.
