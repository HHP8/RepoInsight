# Code of conduct

RepoInsight contributors must communicate respectfully, welcome differing experience levels, focus critique on work rather than people, protect private information, and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled as confidentially and fairly as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
